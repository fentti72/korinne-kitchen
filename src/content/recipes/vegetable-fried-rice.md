---
title: Weeknight Vegetable Fried Rice
category: main-dishes
summary: PLACEHOLDER RECIPE — Korinne will replace this with her own. Day-old rice, a hot pan, and whatever vegetables are in the fridge.
servings: 4
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 15
photo: ../../assets/photos/blog-fried-rice.jpg
photoAlt: Vegetable fried rice with peas, carrots, and corn in a pan with a wooden spoon
tags: [dinner, weeknight]
ingredients:
  - { amount: 4, unit: cups, item: cooked white rice, chilled overnight }
  - { amount: 3, unit: tbsp, item: neutral oil, divided }
  - { amount: 3, item: eggs, lightly beaten }
  - { amount: 1, item: small onion, diced }
  - { amount: 2, item: garlic cloves, minced }
  - { amount: 1.5, unit: cups, item: frozen peas and carrots }
  - { amount: 0.5, unit: cup, item: corn kernels }
  - { amount: 3, unit: tbsp, item: soy sauce }
  - { amount: 1, unit: tsp, item: toasted sesame oil }
  - { amount: 3, item: green onions, sliced }
  - { item: salt and white pepper, to taste }
steps:
  - Break up any clumps in the cold rice with your hands.
  - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Scramble the eggs until just set, then move them to a plate.
  - Add the remaining oil, then the onion. Cook 2–3 minutes until softened. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  - Add the peas, carrots, and corn and cook 2 minutes.
  - Add the rice and spread it across the pan. Let it sit undisturbed for a minute to crisp, then toss. Repeat a few times.
  - Stir in the soy sauce, sesame oil, eggs, and most of the green onions. Season with salt and white pepper.
  - Serve topped with the rest of the green onions.
---
PLACEHOLDER NOTES. Cold rice is the secret: fresh rice steams and turns mushy.
